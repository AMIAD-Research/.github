<a href="https://www.defense.gouv.fr/amiad-agence-ia-defense"><strong>AMIAD</strong></a> (French Ministry of Armed Forces Agency for Artificial Intelligence in Defense), created on May 1st 2024, represents the first concrete step of the Ministry of Armed Forces’ AI strategy announced in March 2024. Its primary mission is to accelerate the development, integration, and large-scale deployment of artificial intelligence technologies in order to enhance operational superiority and improve day-to-day efficiency for all personnel of the Ministry.

Directly attached to the Minister of Armed Forces, AMIAD oversees and ensures the coordination of all AI-related projects across the Ministry. To achieve its objectives, the agency develops central AI expertise and manages a portfolio of projects in close collaboration with the defense AI ecosystem.

AMIAD experts and researchers are distributed between the technical center located in Bruz, near Rennes, and a research activity in Palaiseau on the École polytechnique campus.

AMIAD is committed to strengthening the Ministry of Armed Forces’ ability to integrate and leverage AI technologies in a secure, efficient, and sovereign manner.
